Meet the flock

Meet our beautiful flock.

Two much-loved pet sheep breeds and one critically rare Australian heritage breed, each chosen for temperament, beauty and unique qualities.

Valais Blacknose

Known as the cutest sheep in the world, Valais Blacknose are loved for their striking black markings, woolly faces and gentle nature.

Valais Breed-Up Program

We are part of the Valais Blacknose breed-up journey in Australia, working toward preserving the distinctive appearance, temperament and fibre qualities of this remarkable breed.


Enquire about Valais Blacknose

Babydolls

Often called the smiling teddy bear sheep, Babydolls are small, friendly and full of character. They are a wonderful choice for people looking for charming pet sheep.


Enquire about Babydolls

Elliottdale

A very rare and critical Australian heritage breed, Elliottdales are valued for their history, resilience and distinctive wool qualities.


Enquire about Elliottdales
