About us

A small flock with a big heart.

We are building a boutique sheep breeding program focused on distinctive breeds, gentle temperaments and high-quality fleece.

Our story

Our approach is simple: healthy animals, calm handling, considered breeding and honest communication with future owners.

Whether you are looking for beautiful pet sheep, rare heritage genetics or naturally grown wool, we aim to raise sheep that are well cared for from day one.

Rare and distinctive breeds

From the iconic Valais Blacknose to critically rare Elliottdales, our flock is intentionally unique.

Raised with care

Our sheep are handled calmly and raised with a focus on welfare, health and long-term wellbeing.

Future availability

We plan limited breeding and may offer waitlists for future lambs, wool and breeding stock.
