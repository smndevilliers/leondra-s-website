About the wool side of things

Each of our three breeds produces very different fleece. The Valais wool is coarse and curly, great for felting and rugs. The Babydolls have a soft, fine fleece that hand spinners love. The Elliottdales grow the longest, most lustrous fibre of the three, with a real silky shine to it.

We're still working out which products and approaches make sense for a flock our size. We'll post updates here as we learn more.

If you're a hand spinner, felter or fibre artist and you'd like to know when fleece becomes available, drop us a line.
