About us

A small flock with a big heart.

We are building a boutique sheep breeding program focused on distinctive breeds, gentle temperaments and high-quality fleece. Our approach is simple: healthy animals, calm handling, considered breeding and honest communication with future owners.


Whether you are looking for beautiful pet sheep, rare heritage genetics or naturally grown wool, we aim to raise sheep that are well cared for from day one.

Meet the flock

Meet our beautiful flock.

Our flock includes two much-loved pet sheep breeds and one critically rare Australian heritage breed, each chosen for their temperament, beauty and unique qualities.

Valais Blacknose

Known as the cutest sheep in the world, Valais Blacknose are loved for their striking black markings, woolly faces and gentle nature.


Valais Breed-Up Program

We are part of the Valais Blacknose breed-up journey in Australia, working toward preserving the distinctive appearance, temperament and fibre qualities of this remarkable breed.


Enquire about Valais Blacknose

Babydolls

Often called the smiling teddy bear sheep, Babydolls are small, friendly and full of character. They are a wonderful choice for people looking for charming pet sheep.


Enquire about Babydolls

Elliottdale

A very rare and critical Australian heritage breed, Elliottdales are valued for their history, resilience and distinctive wool qualities.


Enquire about Elliottdales

Frequently asked questions

Common questions from future owners.

Are Valais Blacknose and Babydolls suitable as pet sheep?

Yes. Both breeds are known for their friendly temperaments and are popular as companion and lifestyle sheep when properly cared for.

Do sheep need to live with other sheep?

Yes. Sheep are social animals and should not be kept alone. We recommend keeping at least two compatible sheep together.

Will you offer lambs or breeding stock in the future?

Yes. Future availability and waitlist information will be announced through the Farm Journal section and direct enquiries.

Do you sell wool or fleece?

We plan to offer selected raw fleece and wool products depending on seasonal availability and flock growth.
