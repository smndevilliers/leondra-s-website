Boutique sheep farm

Three sheep breeds, one small farm in the Barossa.

We're raising Valais Blacknose, Babydoll and Elliottdale sheep on a small property in Eden Valley, South Australia.

Each breed was picked on purpose. The Valais for their famously sweet faces, the Babydolls for being the friendliest pet sheep going around, and the Elliottdales because they're a rare Australian breed worth saving.
